# Scoreboard design

Retain the established forest-green identity, locally hosted Inter/Inter Tight typography, paper surfaces and light/dark tokens in `src/app/globals.css`.

Landing pages lead with compact, bold typography and working examples. Product graphics carry the expression: green club standings, warm editorial tables and a dark tournament draw. Avoid decorative gradients, stock imagery and invented statistics. A single reduced-motion-aware entrance is sufficient.

Editors prioritize operation: labelled native inputs, direct row/column controls, a persistent preview on desktop and a preview switch on mobile. Tables scroll inside their own region. Large brackets open at full size with a fit option. Never shrink the entire page to accommodate the graphic.

Use existing Button, Dropdown, theme and language controls. Minimum ordinary touch targets are 44px; compact table controls are at least 36px. Preserve visible keyboard focus, 16px input text, zoom and reduced-motion preferences. Graphic text chooses black or white against user-selected colours for readable contrast. Long values wrap in exported graphics rather than disappearing.

Public introduction routes and editor routes are separate. Existing Scoreboard URLs and its presentation mode remain stable. Example interactions never persist data.
